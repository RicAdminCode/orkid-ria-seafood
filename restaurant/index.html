Finding Orkid Ria today.

Where is Orkid Ria now?

Our current restaurant is at Lot 418, Jalan Pantai Tengah, Langkawi. Older travel guides describe our former Pantai Cenang location. Use our current Google Maps directions for your journey, and visit our story page for the earlier chapter.

What time do you open?

We’re open every day from 1:00 pm to 11:00 pm. If you are planning a late arrival or a visit on a public holiday, call +60 17-614 4128 to check arrangements with the team.

How do I request a table?

Contact us with your preferred date, time and number of guests. A phone or WhatsApp enquiry is a request; please wait for the restaurant to confirm your table. See contact and reservation details.
