### Conflicts resolved for website copy

- **Opening year:** the official tourism guide’s 1989 account is stronger than a directory journal’s “since 2000.” Retain attribution rather than using an unsupported anniversary badge.
- **Reopening:** November 2024 local reporting predates the January 2025 newspaper article. Do not publish “reopened in 2025” or a precise date without records.
- **Address:** older sources refer to Pantai Cenang and differ on Lot 1225/1255. These are historical premises. Keep current navigation directed to Lot 418 in Pantai Tengah. Chiefeater’s prose also differs from its own address panel; use corroborated current address details.
- **Postcode:** the 2024 local report uses 07000; the current website/listings use 07100. The postcode still needs owner confirmation; it was not silently changed from the existing site.
- **Facilities:** “200 guests” is a visitor estimate, not a verified capacity. Parking, accessibility, payment methods, live music schedules and private-event packages need current operational confirmation.
- **Dietary status:** pork-free wording and third-party halal labels are not proof of halal certification. Publish neither certification nor a food-safety guarantee without authoritative evidence.
- **Identity:** no independently verified official Instagram account, current legal company name or founder biography was found. The user-supplied Facebook link stays; Facebook access was temporarily blocked during research.
- **Images:** dates attached to reviews are not camera capture dates. Existing permissions remain unresolved. This work did not obtain a commercial image licence, download additional photos or invent historic imagery.

### Information still needed from the restaurant

1. Founder’s full name, founding location/date, and any records or family photographs explaining the Kuah story and early Pantai Cenang years.
2. Confirmed relocation/reopening date and how the current operation relates to the historic family business.
3. Current legal company name, approved English/Chinese brand wording, logo files and official social account handles.
4. Owner-approved current menu, preparations, pricing basis, seasonal availability and dietary/allergen procedures.
5. Confirmation of address/postcode, contact numbers, WhatsApp availability, opening hours and reservation process.
6. Verified seating/event capacity, parking, step-free access, payment methods and any current entertainment schedule.
7. Written rights to use venue/guest photographs on this website and social media, with any required credits.

These gaps do not prevent the sourced About expansion. They do prevent definitive founder biographies, operational promises and a fully owner-approved launch pack. No businesses or reviewers were contacted and nothing was published to an external service as part of this research.
