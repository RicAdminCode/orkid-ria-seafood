## Press backgrounder

Orkid Ria: Langkawi roots, a Pantai Tengah table

Orkid Ria Seafood Restaurant brings people together over seafood at Lot 418 in Pantai Tengah, Langkawi. Its current setting includes indoor and outdoor dining spaces beside a water feature, with live seafood tanks forming part of the dining experience.

The name has a longer connection with the island. Naturally Langkawi’s Bits & Eats tourism guide records an establishment in Pantai Cenang in 1989 and a rebuild after a fire in 2004. These are attributed historical milestones rather than a claim of uninterrupted trading. In January 2025, The National reported Orkid Ria’s return in Pantai Tengah after a closure of more than four years. That article records the return; it does not establish the precise reopening date.

At today’s restaurant, guests can begin by discussing the available seafood and cooking options with the team. Weight, preparation and price should be confirmed before ordering. The idea is a meal built around the people at the table: seafood selected together, dishes shared, and time to enjoy the setting.

For visitors who remember Orkid Ria in Pantai Cenang, the current Pantai Tengah address is an important distinction. Older travel stories still describe the previous premises. Guests should use the current map pin when planning their journey rather than rely on directions in historical guides.

The restaurant’s published contact number is +60 17-614 4128, and current listings show daily hours of 1 pm to 11 pm. Guests planning a group meal should contact the restaurant with their preferred date, time and party size, and wait for confirmation of their arrangements. Specific seafood availability, dietary requirements and seating preferences are best discussed directly with the team before a visit.

For current visitor information, menu highlights and contact details, visit orkidriaseafood.com. Historical background and present-day dining information should be read separately: the restaurant’s past informs its story, while today’s team can confirm what is available for each visit.

## Outreach sequence

1. Confirm the facts and publish working canonical landing pages.
2. Correct existing relevant listings first, using the approved fact sheet.
3. Send one individually tailored editorial pitch per suitable outlet from an authorized business representative.
4. Offer interview availability and licensed material only when secured. The editor decides whether and how to cover it.

6. Log coverage, factual accuracy, referral enquiries and confirmed bookings. Do not measure success by raw paid-link counts.

## Links and sponsorship

Seek genuine, independently chosen editorial coverage and correct relevant listings. Do not buy ranking links, require keyword-rich anchors or exchange meals/products for followed links. Paid or sponsored coverage must be clearly disclosed and links qualified with rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow". Google specifically identifies manipulative links in distributed press releases/guest posts and low-quality directory links as spam. Use the brand name or natural source attribution if an editor chooses to link. Distribution is for readership and verifiable information, not a promised ranking benefit. [Google policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam)
